# Contributing to Branes.AI

Thank you for your interest in contributing. Before we can merge any contribution, every author must accept the [Branes.AI Individual Contributor License Agreement](https://github.com/branes-ai/cla/blob/main/ICLA.md).

## How to sign

1. Open your pull request. The CLA bot will comment if you have not yet signed.
2. Sign on the [signing page](https://branes-ai.github.io/cla/sign/) (about 2 minutes; your details stay private). The bot's comment has a personal link that fills in your username and pull request.
3. The check re-runs by itself and turns green within a few minutes. One acceptance covers every Branes.AI repository; you will not be asked again.

Comment `recheck` to run the check again.

## Joining Branes.AI as an employee, contractor or intern

Do not sign. Your onboarding agreement covers your work; ask your Branes.AI contact to add your GitHub username to the CLA allowlist. The check re-runs by itself within 15 minutes.

## Contributing for an employer or university

If your employer or university has rights to your work, it must also sign the [Branes.AI Corporate Contributor License Agreement](https://github.com/branes-ai/cla/blob/main/CCLA.md) once and list your GitHub username in Schedule A. Contact kishore.kondapi@branes.ai. You still sign the Individual CLA on the signing page.

## What the agreements mean

The agreements let Branes.AI use, modify, relicense and commercialize contributions, including in proprietary products. You keep the copyright in your contribution, but you receive no royalty, payment or ownership interest in Branes.AI products.

Make sure the email address on your commits is linked to your GitHub account, so the bot can match your commits to your signature.
